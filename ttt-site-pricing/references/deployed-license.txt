Section 6 — Noncompete
================================================================================

Any purpose is a permitted purpose, except for providing to others any
product that competes with TPipe.

A product competes with TPipe if it provides LLM orchestration, multi-agent
pipeline orchestration, or any functionality that overlaps with TPipe's core
purpose as a general-purpose AI agent orchestration layer — regardless of
interface (service, library, plugin, API, CLI), platform, deployment model,
or pricing (free or paid).

A product competes with TPipe even if:
  - it is provided through a different interface or programming language
  - it is ported to a different platform or architecture
  - it is offered free of charge
  - it is deployed as a hosted service rather than distributed as software

Using TPipe internally to build or operate your own product is not
competition. Competition means offering TPipe-like functionality to others
as a product or service.
